Frequently Asked Questions

How is my data stored?

Your calendar and expense records are stored locally on your device.

How can I export my records?

Open the Expenses or Summary tab, select Export, choose a date range, and use the CSV export option.

How do I record an office day?

Open the In-Office Rate tab, select a date, and choose Office, Work From Home, or Off.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to support requests within a few business days.
