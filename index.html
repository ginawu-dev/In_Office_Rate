Hybrid work tracking that fits your pattern

In Office Rate helps you record Office, Work From Home, and Off days while tracking your monthly working-day totals and in-office percentage.

Office, WFH & Off

Log each day as Office, Work From Home, or Off so your work pattern stays clear over time.

Monthly totals

Review monthly working-day totals to understand how often your schedule includes office time.

In-office percentage & target

Calculate your in-office percentage and set a target in-office rate to plan your hybrid routine.

Optional automatic office and WFH detection

  • Save Office and Home locations to help with automatic work-status detection.
  • Office detection requires at least one hour at a saved Office location.
  • Manual entries take priority when a status is recorded directly.
Location-based detection

Automatic detection is optional and can help support your work-status tracking. It does not replace direct manual entry when you prefer to set status yourself.

Holiday planning

Plan date ranges for holidays, and those days are automatically marked as Off. You can still change individual dates when needed.

Plan time off without losing visibility

  • Set holiday date ranges.
  • See holiday countdowns live.
  • Adjust individual dates if your plans change.

Track work expenses with your status records

By date & category

Record expenses by date and category to keep spending organized.

Weekly, monthly & yearly views

Review expenses in weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries to spot trends over time.

Category percentages & CSV export

See category percentages and export your work-status and expense data as CSV.

  • Users who want to see office/WFH patterns over time.

Frequently asked questions

How can I track how many days I work in the office?

Record each day as Office, Work From Home, or Off and review your monthly working-day totals.

Is there an app to calculate my in-office percentage?

Yes. In Office Rate calculates your in-office percentage and lets you set a target in-office rate.

Can I track Office and Work From Home days on iPhone?

Yes. In Office Rate is designed for iPhone and tracks Office, Work From Home, and Off statuses.

Can In Office Rate automatically detect when I am in the office?

Yes, optionally. Users can save Office and Home locations to assist automatic detection, and Office detection requires at least one hour at a saved Office location.

Can I set a target office attendance rate?

Yes. You can set a target in-office rate within the app.

Can I track holidays and days off?

Yes. You can plan holiday date ranges, and those dates are automatically marked as Off, with a live countdown.

Can I track work expenses?

Yes. In Office Rate supports expense tracking by date and category, with weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries.

Can I export my office attendance and expense records?

Yes. CSV export is available for work-status and expense data.

Does In Office Rate sync across devices?

Yes, via optional private iCloud syncing across devices.

Does In Office Rate provide tax or employment advice?

No. The app does not provide tax, financial, or employment advice.
